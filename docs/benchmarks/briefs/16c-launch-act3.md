**Brief:**
Make act 3 of a launch film for a made-up weather app called Drizzle: 1920×1080, 30 fps, 22.6 s. The narration is recorded: place `vo-16-07.wav` at 200 ms, `vo-16-08.wav` at 7400 ms and `vo-16-09.wav` at 14900 ms, whole, tagged as voice. `music-documentary.mp3` is the bed under everything (faded in and out, ducked under the voice). Plan the beats first, then build each shot with one `script_run`.

Shot 1, 0 to 7.2 s, "real physics". The night sky with the horizon glow; the cloud (outline filled lavender-white) above the wordmark "drizzle" (about 200 px, centre). Raindrops (small pale-blue drops, 15 to 25 of them, staggered) fall from the cloud with real physics and land on the actual letter shapes, bouncing and rolling off them. On "They fall too" (5.7 s) the letters themselves drop and tumble onto a floor near the bottom of the frame, piling at odd angles, settled by 7.0 s.

Shot 2, 7.2 to 14.6 s, "a 3D world". On "depth" (8.3 s) cut to a 3D world: the wordmark "drizzle" extruded thick (soft peach-cream material with a rounded bevel), standing on a mirror floor that reflects it, light mist, depth of field, a few small glowing spheres floating around it, and a rose-pink light glowing behind one side. The camera starts low and close on the letters and drifts back and around a little (a slow move that ends square on the word by 14 s). "Same timeline, same keyframes" plays over the drift.

Shot 3, 14.6 to 22.6 s, "it gets a face". The navy sky with small soft dots of light floating. A big lavender-white cloud in the centre (about 600 px wide), no face yet. On "It blinks on its own" (19.1 s) it starts blinking and breathing by itself (bring the character to life). On "Nobody asked it to" (20.5 s) it gives a pleased squint and a small head tilt.
