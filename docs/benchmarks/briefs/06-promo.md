# 06 · Product promo (vertical, 15 s)

**Brief:**
Make a 15-second 1080×1920 promo for "Aurora" wireless earbuds using the three product photos and the logo in the project. Hook in the first 1.5 s with a bold three-word claim, then three feature beats (30-hour battery, active noise cancelling, IPX5 sweat-proof), then the offer "$79 — launch week only" with the price in the accent colour, and end on the logo with "Shop now at aurora.audio". Energetic, with music-like pacing. The voiceover lines and a music bed are in the project; build the piece around them.

**AtomCut's recipe (the bar):** hook (0–1.5 s): product image `pop-in` with a bold 3-word claim; features (1.5–11 s): three 3-second beats, each a photo plus one line, `push` transitions; offer (11–13.5 s): price in the accent, `punch`; end (13.5–15 s): logo and CTA, hold; music with beats on cuts; pops on each text entrance.

**Media:** `product-1.png`, `product-2.png`, `product-3.png` (1080×1080), `logo.png`, the voice lines `vo-1-hook.wav`, `vo-2-battery.wav`, `vo-3-anc.wav`, `vo-4-sweat.wav`, `vo-5-offer.wav`, `vo-6-end.wav` and the music bed `music-promo.mp3`, imported by the runner before the brief.
