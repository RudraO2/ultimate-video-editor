# 07 · Logo reveal

**Brief:**
Make a 4-second 1920×1080 logo reveal for "Northwind Studio". The mark is a simple geometric one: a circle with a diagonal line through it. Draw the mark on, fill it, sweep a shine across, then bring in the wordmark letter by letter. Dark background, one accent colour.

**AtomCut's recipe (the bar):** the mark as path layers (`d`), strokes only, `draw` in (900 ms, staggered 120 ms); fills fade in over the strokes (300 ms) as the strokes fade out; one `shine` sweep; the wordmark `unblur-up` per letter; a single low `pop` on the fill.

**Media:** none.
