# 03 · Kinetic typography

**Brief:**
Make a 6-second 1080×1920 kinetic type piece for the line "Make every second count". One word at a time, huge and centred, on the beat (about 120 bpm), with varied entrances and the word "count" as the colour-flashed key word. Bold, high-contrast palette. Cut it to the music in the project (120 bpm).

**AtomCut's recipe (the bar):** one word at a time at 20–30U, centred, each word its own layer (or a caption with `maxWords: 1`); entrances alternate (`punch`, `slide-up`, `drop`) on the beat; the key word flashes colour; motion blur on; a pop or thock on each word.

**Media:** `music-kinetic.mp3`, imported by the runner before the brief.
