Part 4 of 4 of the launch-film recreation (see 16a for the shared look: navy night sky, low rose-amber horizon glow, "drizzle" in Fredoka 700 cream #F6E7D2, a lavender-white cloud, captions in DM Sans).

**Brief:**
Make act 4 of a launch film for a made-up weather app called Drizzle: 1920×1080, 30 fps, 39.0 s. The narration is recorded: place `vo-16-10.wav` at 200 ms, `vo-16-11.wav` at 7900 ms, `vo-16-12.wav` at 12600 ms, `vo-16-13.wav` at 17500 ms and `vo-16-14.wav` at 23000 ms, whole, tagged as voice. `music-documentary.mp3` is the bed under everything (faded in and out, ducked under the voice; let it swell a little in the last 3 s). Plan the beats first, then build each shot with one `script_run`.
