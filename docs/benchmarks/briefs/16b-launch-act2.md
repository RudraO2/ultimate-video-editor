# 16b · Launch film, act 2: the studio and the build begins

Part 2 of 4 of the launch-film recreation (see 16a for the shared look: navy night sky, low rose-amber horizon glow, "drizzle" in Fredoka 700 cream #F6E7D2, a lavender-white cloud, captions in DM Sans).

**Brief:**
Make act 2 of a launch film for a made-up weather app called Drizzle: 1920×1080, 30 fps, 32.2 s. The narration is recorded: place `vo-16-04.wav` at 200 ms, `vo-16-05.wav` at 7400 ms and `vo-16-06.wav` at 15600 ms, whole, tagged as voice. `music-documentary.mp3` is the bed under everything (faded in and out, ducked under the voice). Plan the beats first, then build each shot with one `script_run`.

Shot 1, 0 to 7.0 s, "this is the editor". Near-black ground with a faint violet vignette. On "Ultimate Video Editor" (0.6 s) a glowing app mark (an icon of a film frame or a play button, in a rose-to-amber gradient) blooms in at the centre with the name beneath. On "a whole motion studio" (2.3 s) it gives way to `uve-editor.png` (a screenshot of this editor) shown as a rounded window with a soft shadow, easing up from 92 % to 100 % scale. On "Free." (5.1 s) and "No account." (5.9 s) two small dark pills with those words pop in at the window's top-right corner.

Shot 2, 7.0 to 15.2 s, "an app that doesn't exist". The night sky with the horizon glow. On "Drizzle" (11.2 s) the app icon pops in at the centre: a rounded square with a violet-to-indigo gradient and a white cloud icon, "Drizzle" in white beneath it. On "It tells you" (12.0 s) a notification card slides down from the top: white, rounded, the small app icon, "Drizzle" bold, "now" grey on the right, and the line "Rain in 12 minutes. Maybe bring a coat." On "Groundbreaking." (14.0 s) the card tips a few degrees with a dry little tick, a deadpan beat.

Shot 3, 15.2 to 32.2 s, "it starts with a word". The sky alone, the glow brighter. On "a word" (16.3 s) "drizzle" types itself in letter by letter at the centre (large, about 200 px) with a blinking caret and soft key sounds. It sits still through "just sits there". On "a little motion" (19.8 s) the letters do a quick per-letter wave or bounce. On "borrow a cloud" (23.4 s) a row of cloud icons from different icon sets slides in from the right above the word, each with its set's name in small grey mono type beneath it, glides left and slows so one outlined cloud stops in the middle while the others fade away (search the icons; 6 to 10 sets). On "draws itself in" (27.5 s) the chosen cloud's outline draws on, then fills lavender-white. On "A cloud should rain" (29.0 s) the cloud fattens slightly; on "So it rains" (30.2 s) a few drops (small elongated ellipses) fall from it towards the word.
