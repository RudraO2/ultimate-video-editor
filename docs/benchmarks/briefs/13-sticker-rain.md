# 13 · Sticker rain

**Brief:**
Make a 6-second 1920×1080 clip for a launch party: the words "WE'RE LIVE" sit big in the middle of a deep navy frame, then a shower of party emoji stickers rains down from above, bouncing and tumbling, piling on top of the words and on the bottom of the frame without leaving the picture. Halfway through, a gust of wind shoves the pile to one side. Let everything settle before the end.

**AtomCut's recipe (the bar):** about 20 transparent stickers (one consistent look) as active bodies, started above the frame at staggered heights so they land in a cascade, each with a small random spin (±90°/s) and sideways speed (±150 px/s); the headline a passive collider so stickers land on its letters; walls on so nothing leaves the frame; a wind force whose strength is keyed from 0 up to about 700 and back for a short gust on a beat; about a second of settle before the cut; impact sounds on the first landings, kept quiet.

**Media:** none.
