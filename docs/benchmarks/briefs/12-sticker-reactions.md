# 12 · Sticker reactions

**Brief:**
Make a 6-second 1080×1920 vertical clip announcing "We shipped v2!". On a warm cream background, the headline lands first; then, one beat at a time, playful emoji-style stickers react around it: something celebrating, something fast, something hot, and an approving hand to close. Each sticker should feel like it pops onto the screen. Keep it tidy: the headline stays the focal point.

**AtomCut's recipe (the bar):** pictures with transparent grounds placed as media layers (AtomCut generates them; here they come from the sticker library), one consistent look across all of them (all 3D), each entering with `pop-in` (overshoot) on its own beat, staggered rather than all at once, each with a `pop` on its own sounds stack, sized so none softens (3D stickers at most about 300 px), nothing over the headline, exits quicker than entrances.

**Media:** none.
